# BlindGuide

An audio-first AI assistant for blind and low-vision people. It runs on a phone or smart glasses, sees through the camera, listens through the microphone and guides the user by voice.

## Principles
- **Safety first:** BlindGuide supports the cane or guide dog and never replaces them. It reports what it sees and never gives false reassurance.
- **Honest about uncertainty:** it says "I'm not sure" instead of guessing.
- **Privacy:** no recordings are stored by default, and bystanders' faces are never saved.
- **Built with blind users:** co-design and field testing from day one.
